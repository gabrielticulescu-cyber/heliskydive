Not a skydive. An expedition.

Most jumps start at an airfield and end over farmland. Ours start in a helicopter climbing past forested ridges, with the Făgăraș peaks on the horizon and Transylvania laid out below.

No aircraft door to dive from, no queue for the next load. You climb out, hang from the skid, and let go when you're ready. Then you fall over some of the wildest country left in Europe.

    Come for the jump. Stay for the mountains.

    Fly into Sibiu and spend a few days in the Carpathians. We plan the jump, the stay and the days around it, so all you need to bring is your gear.

    1. Arrive in Sibiu

      Pickup from the airport, dinner in the old town, gear check and briefing.

    2. Jump day

      Out to the zone in the morning. Jump, repack, jump again while the weather holds.

    3. Into the mountains

      Horse riding, ATV trails or heli-biking, then a mountain dinner and a night in a cabin.

    4. Last look

      One more jump if you want it, then back to Sibiu for your flight home.

    How a heli jump works

    From the briefing to the landing field, a jump day runs like this.

    1. Briefing

      Gear check, exit order, landing plan and the weather call for the day.

    2. The climb

      The helicopter lifts off from the landing field and climbs over the ridges to 3000–4000 m.

    3. The skid

      You climb out, hang from the skid and let go when you're ready.

    4. Freefall

      50 to 60 seconds of freefall from 4000 m, with the Carpathians below you.

    5. Canopy and landing

      Open the parachute, fly down over the valley and land where the ground crew is waiting.

    Questions

    Who can jump tandem?

    Anyone from 14 years old in normal health. Under 18 you need written consent from both parents with a certified signature. Over 60 you need a medical certificate. Every tandem passenger fills in a health declaration before the flight, and tells us their weight when they sign up.

    What do licensed jumpers need?

    A valid skydiving licence, a valid medical certificate, your logbook, and gear with valid airworthiness documents. We check them on the landing field before boarding.

    What if the weather is bad?

    We only fly when wind, cloud and visibility are inside the limits for your level. If they're not, we move your jump to another day.

    Will my jump be filmed?

    Tandem jumps are filmed from start to finish: on the ground, in the helicopter and in the air, where two camera flyers jump alongside you. You get edited photos and video afterwards. Our camera flyers have more than 200 jumps each, as Romanian rules require.

    Can friends or family come along?

    Yes. Companions can ride along in the helicopter and watch you step off the skid, stay the night in the mountains with you, or both. Choose the companion options when you join the wishlist.
